Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026. Last updated: 18 October 2026.

In short: Gravity Defied Reincarnation does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no account and no network requests of its own. Your records and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Gravity Defied Reincarnation (the "game") is published by Akademic Games (Yevhenii Horpyniak), the developer. Contact: yevhop@gmail.com.

The game is an unofficial fan remake. It is not affiliated with Codebrew Software.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect personal data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, files or usage statistics. It does not ask you to create an account or to sign in. It contains no advertising and no analytics or crash reporting tools from third parties.

Network access

The game's own code makes no network requests and sends no data anywhere. On Google Play, purchases and the check whether the full version is owned go through Google Play Billing, the library Google provides for in-app purchases, and the Google Play app on your device. Google operates that library, and its connection to Google is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What is saved on your device

The game saves a small file on your device with your best times, the tracks you have finished, your chosen bike, mode and track, your language, sound and volume settings, your control settings, and on Google Play whether the full version is unlocked. This file never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. On Android you can also delete it with "Clear storage" in the app settings.

If your device or your account makes backups of app data through the system (for example Android backup), that is handled by the system and its provider under their own terms. The game does not take part in it.

Purchases

On Google Play, the full version is a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google collects payment and purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms. The game never sees your payment details. The game only receives from Google Play the information whether the full version is owned, and keeps that fact on your device.

On Steam, purchases and your Steam account are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy. The game does not receive your payment details.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no user profiles, no online features and no ads.

Third parties

The game does not share data with third parties because it has no data to share. The only third parties involved are the stores you use to get the game (Google Play and Steam), which act under their own privacy policies.

Your choices and rights

Because the game keeps no data about you outside your own device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control everything the game saves, and you can remove it at any time by uninstalling the game. If you have a question, write to yevhop@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date at the top. The effective date of this version is 18 October 2026.

Questions and answers

How do I control the bike?
  • Keyboard: W or Up arrow is gas, S or Down arrow is brake, A or Left arrow and D or Right arrow lean the rider back and forward. R restarts the run, Esc or P pauses. All keys can be changed in the settings. The numpad key sets 1, 2 and 3 of the phone original are also available.
  • Gamepad: right trigger or A is gas, left trigger or B is brake, the left stick or the D-pad leans the rider. Start pauses and Y restarts.
  • Touch (phones and tablets): the left side leans the rider back and forward, the right side is brake and gas. You can choose on-screen buttons or touch zones in the controls settings. With zones, holding both bottom zones at once brakes. In the settings you can also assign a movement to each zone and put the speedometer on the left or the right.
Where are my records and settings saved?
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\GravityDefiedReincarnation
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/GravityDefiedReincarnation
  • Android: in the private storage of the app on your phone. It is not visible to other apps. Uninstalling the game or choosing "Clear storage" in the app settings deletes it.

The file is called progress.json. Nothing is sent to any server.

How do I restore my purchase on Android?
Open the purchase page in the game (choose a locked track) and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Google account on the device that was used to buy the game. If it still does not unlock, check your connection to Google Play, wait a minute and try again, then write to us.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by the store where you bought the game, under its rules. On Google Play, open your order history and use the refund option, or see the Google Play refund help. On Steam, see the Steam refund page. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, because the payment goes through the store.
Does the game collect my data?
No. Read the privacy policy.
